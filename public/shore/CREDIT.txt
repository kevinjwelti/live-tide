beach.jpg is that photo rotated so the shoreline runs horizontally, then cropped from the dry sand through the shallow water, stopping before the offshore sandbar. A short continuation of the dry sand is folded onto the sand side so the high-tide peak still has beach under it. The preview shifts each column vertically, at 1:1, so the photo's own foam sits on the tide curve: water above, sand below. It is never enlarged and it does not tile.
