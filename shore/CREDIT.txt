This is the default page for every beach. ?scene=tropic opts back to the turquoise water and rippled sand. ?shore=classic keeps the drawn chart.
Top-down shallow turquoise water over pale sand, with palm shadows across the sand. A swimmer in the source frame was painted out with a nearby patch of water.
Source page: https://www.pexels.com/photo/green-palm-tree-casting-shadow-on-beach-shore-5231851/
Photographer: Jess Loiterton
Image: https://images.pexels.com/photos/5231851/pexels-photo-5231851.jpeg
File: scene/lagoon.jpg, the left portion of that photograph, with the swimmer removed.
The sand and the palm shadow are the photograph itself. They are not redrawn, and they are not warped. The shadow in the photograph is the tree's own soft shadow. A wash is drawn only along the waterline: irregular foam from layered noise, a thin sheet of water, and a darker wet band that lingers as the water slips back. One wash takes about 10 seconds. A slower second wash changes how far each set reaches, so they are not identical. The water's movement is the same kind of noise, a few pixels, only on the turquoise side of the line. It is not a video of water. A calm top-down lagoon clip that matched this photograph was not available, and pasting a different sea onto it looked like a second picture.
